I design gameplay systems, combat and levels, and I build them myself in Blueprints and C#.

I am a technical game designer with 5+ years of experience in Unity and Unreal Engine. When I design a mechanic or a system, I work out its details and depth, prototype it and make my decisions from how it feels in play. I started with mobile games: many prototypes and one soft-launched management game, Fancy Restaurant. I now focus on PC and console games in Unreal Engine.
